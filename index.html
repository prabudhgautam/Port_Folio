Primary Project: Franky AMR

I built Franky as a way to understand mobile robotics from the physical robot model all the way to autonomous navigation.

I started with the differential-drive kinematics and URDF model, then worked through wheel velocity control, odometry, TF2 transform trees, and RViz visualization in ROS 2. The interesting part wasn't just getting the robot to move—it was figuring out why frames, odometry, controllers, and sensors weren't behaving as expected, and learning to debug each layer of the stack independently.

ROS VERSION:ROS 2 Jazzy
SIMULATOR:Gazebo Sim
CONTROL:ros2_control
TRANSFORMS:TF2 Tree
DIMENSIONS:r=0.033m | b=0.170m

Current Implementation Status

The platform currently supports teleoperation, odometry broadcasting, TF2 frame transforms, and simulation-based navigation workflows. Ray-cast 2D LiDAR sensors and wheel encoders feed the control loop, with active extension underway toward full SLAM mapping and Nav2 autonomous path planning In Active Development.

Lessons Learned & Solved Bugs

  • Mesh Origin vs. TF Calibration: Solved visual mesh misalignments by re-calculating geometry relative to CAD origins rather than guessing visual rotations.
  • Friction & Controller Tuning: Adjusted surface friction and command rates inside Gazebo physics tags to keep wheel encoders aligned with odometry calculations during sharp turns.
